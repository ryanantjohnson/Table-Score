# TableRec bookkeeping setup

Recommendation as researched November 15, 2026: Wave Starter for free bookkeeping, bills and manual transaction records. Automated bank imports/receipt capture are paid features. Zoho Books Free is another option under $50K annual revenue, but its current US plan comparison reserves unpaid vendor Bills for Professional and higher.

No accounting account has been created, bank connected, money moved, or tax return prepared.

## Suggested records
- Business checking (when opened); business credit card if used.
- Owner contributions (equity), owner distributions (equity), and a separate owner-loan liability only if funds actually are a documented loan. Money put into the company is separate from customer revenue.
- Revenue: Apple Pro, Google Pro, Restaurant subscriptions; future merchandise separately.
- Separate settlement/clearing accounts for Apple and Google. Reconcile sales statements, store commissions, refunds, adjustments and payouts so a net bank deposit is not mistaken for the full sales history. Gross/net revenue presentation should be decided with the accountant under store agreements.
- Expenses: hosting/database, maps/API services, software tools, developer accounts, domain/email, legal/formation, marketing, professional services. Preserve original receipts and business-use percentage. Startup/development costs may need different tax treatment: do not automatically deduct every prelaunch payment.
- Capture personal-paid business costs with receipt date, vendor, amount, category, business purpose and paid-by owner. Avoid counting the same receipt again when reimbursed.
- Bills: record due date, frequency, amount, autopay status and renewal/cancellation dates. Reconcile once a month.

## Starting costs to verify from actual statements
Apple developer program, Google Play registration, Supabase, Google Maps/API, domain/email, development software, legal/LLC fees. Historical conversation figures are not receipts and must not be imported as verified transactions.

## Taxes
Bookkeeping supplies profit/loss and balance sheet data; it is not a complete personalized tax estimate. Entity election, household wages/withholding, filing status, deductible startup costs, income, and Texas obligations affect the calculation. Use a CPA-reviewed estimate when revenue begins; do not treat a flat percentage as a tax bill. The IRS self-employed tax center and Publication 505 explain net profit and estimated payments. Keep tax reserves separate from spendable cash once a target is established.
